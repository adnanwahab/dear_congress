## Consent, assignment and privacy contract

Use the same neutral study invitation for everyone who encounters it. No outreach
is authorized by this document. For the first pilot, enroll self-attested adults
only without collecting birth dates or identity evidence; do not infer age.
Provide equal, accessible Join and Continue without study controls, unchecked
consent and an explanation before any assignment or research collection:

> Optional homepage wording study. We compare two factual explanations, ask four
> questions, and record whether you open a sample proposal within one minute.
> Joining does not vote, publish anything or contact an office. We collect no
> political views or journal text. You can skip, stop or remove your study record.
> You can use the beta without joining.

Before launch, supplement this with the reviewed operator/contact, exact dates,
retention and deletion limits below. A private withdrawal channel must exist;
never direct someone to post a study token in a public GitHub issue. Joining must
not depend on agreeing with a proposal, publishing, logging in or accepting
marketing. Decline creates no experiment record, token, storage or assignment.
Ordinary operational requests may still be logged under the existing privacy
notice; do not claim zero website logging.

After explicit consent, create a research-only cryptographically random opaque
token and assign A/B with a fresh uniform random bit (50/50). Store the assignment
only in the isolated study store; keep the token in page memory. No IP hashing,
fingerprinting, geography, referrals, cookies, GitHub identity, journal data,
prior civic activity, demographic fields or inferred stance enters assignment.
Use the same assignment through that study tab's sequence; reload ends it rather
than silently creating a new record. Explain that reopening requires fresh consent
and may create a repeat session. Count consenting sessions, never unique people.
Do not add cross-device or cross-session deduplication to hide that limitation.

Use an isolated study build with the pinned public sample fixture and read-only
destination previews. Intercept both destination links without changing their
visible wording; clearly disclose the research preview before enrollment.
No vote, journal, live-round, sponsor-outreach or repository-write API may be
called by the study. Ordinary site access remains available outside the study.
This limits generalization to production and must appear in the report.

Allowed private record: protocol/build/content revisions, arm, opaque token,
consent version, assigned/exposed booleans, four fixed-choice answers or missing,
task-start boolean, elapsed time rounded to seconds and bounded to 0–60,
withdrawal state and a fixed technical-failure code. Keep consent/expiry times
privately for deletion; never publish row-level timestamps or tokens. No free
text, proposal/stance identifiers, full URLs, query strings, browser headers,
contact details, civic receipt IDs or persistent analytics identifiers. Reject
unknown fields. Research requests must omit civic credentials; verify separate
origin/storage and proxy/access-log behavior before promising separation.

Provide Stop and Delete my study record throughout and a private deletion
capability retained by the participant until expiry. Withdrawal deletes the
individual record and cached exports, and recomputes unpublished aggregates;
do not store a participant-linked withdrawal ledger. Failed deletion must show
failure and a private recovery path. For the pilot, no study-store backups:
verify that operational logging and hosting do not silently retain payloads.
Delete raw study data 14 days after collection closes, before public reporting;
disable record intake at closure. Explain that published aggregates cannot be
individually unwound after raw deletion. If those limits cannot be enforced,
do not enroll. Existing civic records/backups are outside this study and unchanged.

## Preregistered measures and analysis

Freeze protocol, copy, sample fixture, build SHA, start/end UTC times and named
human owner in a reviewed repository revision before the first participant.
No recruitment or collection until those fields are completed. Proposed fixed
cap: 200 consenting assigned sessions or 14 consecutive days, whichever first.
This is a feasibility pilot, with no claim of power for a small effect. Do not
extend the window to achieve a favorable result or aspirational adoption target.

At visible variant render start a 60-second wall-clock window. Keep the same
questionnaire and positions in both arms. Ask these fixed-choice questions
without corrective feedback until the observation window ends; allow Skip:

1. Is this beta an official government ballot? Yes / **No** / Not sure.
2. What does opening a proposal do? **Shows it without saving a vote** /
   Saves a vote / Contacts an office / Not sure.
3. Do sample proposal counts show verified resident participation? Yes /
   **No** / Not sure.
4. Who can read a journal excerpt after you publish it? Only me /
   **The public** / Only an official / Not sure.

Bold marks the scoring key here only, never in the participant questionnaire.
Do not ask for party, political stance, voting eligibility or intended vote.
Questions are a comprehension instrument, not a civic correctness score.

| Measure | Frozen definition |
| --- | --- |
| Primary: first-minute comprehension | Sessions with all four first submitted answers correct within 60 seconds / all consented assigned sessions remaining after withdrawal. Skip, no response, timeout and non-rendering assignment score zero. No answer changes used for scoring. |
| Secondary: task start | Sessions that activate the primary CTA and successfully render the pinned read-only proposal preview within 60 seconds / the same denominator. Count once; clicks alone, secondary CTA, reloads, tour starts, votes and publication do not qualify. |
| Diagnostic only | Number exposed, questionnaire completed, missing each answer, technical failures and secondary navigation. Report separately; no post-hoc responder-only winner. |

The clock continues if the tab is hidden. An early exit yields missing/zero
unless the session is withdrawn, in which case remove it entirely. Questions may
be completed before or after a task start in the same window. Preserve the token
in memory through the preview and return; do not add a query parameter or alter
the proposal identity. Measurement failures remain in denominators, separately
flagged. Do not substitute server activity counts for absent study events.

Report A and B numerator/denominator, rates, and B-minus-A percentage-point
differences for both measures. Use 95% Wilson intervals for each rate and a
Newcombe interval for each difference, with a reviewed analysis implementation
and version recorded before enrollment. No subgroup analyses, political segments,
multiple outcome fishing or efficacy peeking. Inspect unblinded data only for
safety/technical validity; do not stop early for favorable performance.

No automatic winner or automatic homepage change. A candidate for a later
human-reviewed implementation requires primary improvement of at least 5
percentage points with a difference interval entirely above zero, secondary
difference interval lower bound above -5 percentage points, and no unresolved
safety/accessibility failure. Otherwise report inconclusive or unfavorable,
including mixed results. These pilot thresholds are proposed decision rules,
not evidence that this sample will resolve them. No inference beyond volunteer
sessions using this instrument and frozen preview.

## Stop rules and report

Immediately halt new enrollment and collection for any unconsented record,
research/civic identity join, unexpected sensitive field, failed withdrawal,
changed political claims between arms, accidental vote/publication/outreach,
official-endorsement confusion identified in review, or inaccessible consent/exit.
Stop for any build/content/protocol mismatch or cross-arm contamination. Revert
the study entry to ordinary access; do not erase civic data. Quarantine affected
research records privately, investigate, honor deletion, and document the incident.
Restart only under a new reviewed protocol revision; do not pool incompatible runs.

Technical checks after the first 20 assigned sessions and each subsequent 20:
pause if more than 5% have assignment/render/recording failures or either arm
has less than 30% of assignments. These are conservative operational pause rules,
not significance tests or outcome-based stopping. Report every pause and its
denominator. A predeclared end/cap always closes enrollment. Never keep a study
running because engagement or user counts are below a desired headline.

Publish a human-reviewed aggregate report in this file or a linked repository
artifact after raw-data expiry, including stopped, null and negative studies.
Publish no cell with fewer than 10 retained sessions; suppress linked totals
that permit reconstruction. With insufficient safe cells, publish “insufficient
data” and the protocol/status/limitations rather than exact counts or rates.
No row exports or fine-grained day/device/region breakdowns. Public publication
is a separate maintainer action, not authorized external messaging by this review.
