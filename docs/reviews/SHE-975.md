- [ ] Record the existing release hold without enabling it or altering settings.
  No automatic merge, quorum reduction, auth/check relaxation or production-data

  independent review/checks and a human-published tagged release; freeze exceptions
  do not bypass those controls. Rollback requires separate human authorization.

A failed gate means HOLD before T0. Any exception record needs incident/evidence,
exact revision and scope, risk, checks, reviewer, owner decision and UTC time.
No broad standing permission follows from an exception.

## Required checkpoints

Every checkpoint collects all five operational reports, even when unchanged.
Record actual UTC time, reporter, evidence period, missing signals, incident IDs,
owner decision (GO / HOLD / CONTINUE / ROLLBACK requested), rationale and next
review. Missed checkpoints or absent evidence remain pending; never backfill a pass.

| Checkpoint | Required review | Release owner action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T-30 | Roster/backup acceptance, freeze acknowledgments, exact candidate and prior image, required checks/review, alerts and measured baseline | Record GO or HOLD before T0; missing gates block launch |
| T+15 | Served `/version` matches approved SHA across available machines; HTTP and desktop/mobile parity reports; health/error signals and first moderation/support/content reports | Confirm rollout acceptance or HOLD and assess rollback |
| T+60 | Compare latency/errors/resource pressure with baseline; persistence incident reports, alert delivery, moderation/support backlog and misleading copy | Record CONTINUE or incident decision; keep freeze |
| T+180 | Repeat all five reports, trend and mitigation review, capacity/cost limits and responder availability | Record CONTINUE/HOLD or authorized rollback decision; confirm remaining coverage |
| T+360 | Final identity/parity and trend review, unresolved incidents, report backlog, follow-up owners and accepted next shift | Explicitly lift or extend freeze with UTC end/review time; hand off responsibility |

Use [release parity](../release-parity.md) for read-only HTTP/browser gates; failed
or missing reports mean NO-GO for acceptance. `/healthz` alone proves liveness,
not persistence or correct votes. Do not cast production test votes or reset data.
Collect aggregate/redacted operational evidence only. Agree thresholds and sample
windows at T-30 from measured workload; January readiness targets are proposals,
not measured capacity. Unknown performance evidence is a gap, not a passing result.

## Incident and rollback decision

Immediately escalate wrong revision/source identity, lost/duplicated writes,
repository/item-kind or PR revision mixing, privacy/auth failure, harmful content,
failed parity, persistent outage or breached agreed performance limits. Each role
may raise an incident; only the human decision owner decides release disposition.

Record detection UTC, impact and evidence references; current SHA/image and last
known compatible image; whether continuing risks data; owner name, decision UTC,
rationale and exact authorized scope. The safe default is HOLD new changes while
investigating. Rollback is never automatic. An old image must be compatible with
current storage; image rollback must preserve all current votes, records and
receipts, not restore an older database snapshot or rewrite source identities.
If compatibility is unknown, record HOLD and obtain a human recovery plan.

After separately authorized execution, the deploy operator records actual image,
SHA, app/machines and times; performance lead reruns read-only health and parity
against the restored image's original SHA. Owner records recovery success or
continued incident and next review. No task here executes this procedure.
