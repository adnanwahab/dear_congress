## Readout and decision

Civic Synthesis is an independent advisory beta, not election infrastructure.
No official endorsement, representative participation or verified resident
eligibility is established. One million users remains a hypothesis, not an
observed result. No synthetic votes are counted in this report.

The actual launch start, exact deployed revision for that window, six-hour
measurement exports, incident history and billing evidence were not supplied or
retrieved. Consequently the requested T+6h adoption figures are **unavailable**,
not zero. A current page load, screenshot, successful test or merged PR cannot
establish historical adoption. The November 13 launch policy date and later
release history do not establish the measurement start.

| Required measure | T+6h result | Evidence and accounting limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pageviews | Unavailable | Source supports aggregate accepted browser route events by Chicago calendar day; no window export available. Reloads can count again; views are not people. |
| Anonymous sessions | Unavailable | Pageview rows have no visitor or session key. Voting identities only cover participating identities and cannot stand in for browsing sessions. |
| Verified people | Unavailable | Anonymous cookies are not verified people. Approved GitHub account ballots are a separate electorate and do not verify unique human beings, residents or eligibility. |
| Advisory choices | Unavailable | Daily distinct identity/scope/target activity exists in source, but no launch export or QA/bot exclusion evidence is available. This is neither a current-choice tally nor a total of every save. |
| Completion funnel | Unavailable at every stage | No linked session-level progression from entry → proposal viewed → accepted advisory choice → draft prepared → share exists in the launch stats schema. Route totals cannot supply conversion or drop-off. |
| Share engagement | Unavailable | Local card/copy/download behavior is not proof of delivery, recipient views or downstream engagement; no engagement export is available. |
| Failures | Unavailable | No time-bounded request/error/incident export; liveness alone cannot establish successful voting, persistence or availability over six hours. |
| Costs | Unavailable | No window-attributed Fly, storage, network, model-provider or monitoring usage/billing export. No cost per user or completion can be calculated. |
| Open risks | Unresolved | Missing window/provenance, incomplete collection, unknown automation/QA contamination, unverified identity, funnel/share gaps, storage and capacity limits; detailed below. |

**Recommendation: hold growth expansion pending evidence and human review.**
This is a recommendation, not an operational change or a claim that the site
failed. Prioritize recovering the window and source evidence, accounting for
synthetic/QA traffic, measuring reliability and cost, then deciding whether a
bounded next cohort fits reviewed capacity and spend limits. Do not use missing
data as a reason to advertise success or failure, increase writer replicas, or
target people by political stance.

### What the metrics can and cannot establish

- `src/server/launch-stats.ts` uses separate SQLite tables. Views have only
  `day, route, count`; vote activity has only `day, identity, scope, target`.
  `snapshot()` exposes today's Chicago calendar day, not arbitrary intervals.
  `startedAt` is collection initialization, not launch time or continuous uptime.
  Exact T+6h reconstruction is impossible from these tables alone when the
  window covers part of a day. Subtracting two daily vote totals is also unsafe:
  an identity/target already seen before launch will deduplicate during launch.
  Midnight resets and repeated targets across dates create further differences.
- The pageview client omits credentials and stores no visitor identity. Same-path
  history changes are suppressed; new loads and pathname transitions can count.
  Blocked JavaScript, failed delivery, rate limits and spoofed events affect
  coverage. Pageviews cannot be divided into anonymous sessions retrospectively.
- Votes deduplicate by day, hashed identity, scope and target; repository targets
  retain item number and exact version. Replacements count once for that key;
  withdrawals do not erase participation. Cross-scope identities cannot be added
  and called unique people. PR revisions must not be collapsed into one ballot.
- `observePublicResponse` counts successful civic and citizen advisory saves;
  repository callbacks separately observe community and approved-account ballots.
  The observer allowlist does not include `/api/live/:code/vote`, so live-round
  activity is not covered by these counters. Goal votes and priority allocation
  are separate activity types, not extra people or interchangeable stance votes.
- Seeded and modeled populations do not emit these vote events. Journals retain
  real/sample/simulated categories. Nevertheless accepted automated public API
  writes are indistinguishable from human writes in the stats store. There is no
  event-level synthetic/QA provenance for votes. Do not call every recorded save
  organic adoption or claim that all synthetic traffic has been excluded.
- Collection is best effort after application persistence, without a shared
  transaction. A crash or disk error can undercount. The incomplete-collection
  flag is process-local and resets on restart. A currently healthy page cannot
  prove uninterrupted historical collection; old records are not backfilled.
- Share cards include curated fictional sample content and optional stance;
  the card explicitly is not proof of a saved vote. The documented share flow
  adds no tracking or automatic posting. Downloads and clipboard success cannot
  establish external engagement.

## Evidence needed to finish the actual report

1. The launch owner confirms `T0` in UTC, offset/local timezone, origin, app,
   deployed commit/image and any intervening release/restart. Define the report
   interval as `[T0, T0 + 6 hours)` using elapsed hours, including across midnight
   or DST. Record extraction time separately from observation time. Current
   `/version` can corroborate today's source but cannot prove the earlier one.
2. Obtain authorized read-only exports with source, interval, coverage, units,
   exclusions and a reproducible aggregation description. Preserve a restricted
   source reference/hash; publish only aggregates. Do not expose tokens, raw
   cookie/account IDs, IPs, journal content or political preferences. Daily-only
   counters must be labeled daily context, never relabeled as six-hour results.
3. Keep civic advisory, repository community, GitHub approved-account, live-round,
   journal, sample and simulation measures distinct. Preserve repository/item
   kind and PR head/base version. Separate operational activity, known test/bot
   traffic and any defensibly attributable non-test activity. If contamination
   cannot be separated, leave adoption unavailable and describe the mixed raw
   count separately. Never delete existing ballots to clean a readout.
4. If an already-approved source supplies anonymous session events, disclose its
   session definition, timeout, coverage and inability to identify people. For
   each funnel stage report the eligible denominator, numerator and same-window
   linkage; use `unavailable` if linkage is missing and `N/A` for a zero
   denominator. Never infer a funnel from independent route totals or silently
   introduce identifiers, tracking, fingerprinting or cross-system linkage.
5. Distinguish share initiation/copy/download from confirmed delivery, recipient
   visits and completion. Only report stages actually observed. Do not send
   messages, initiate outreach or infer shares from URL appearances to fill gaps.
6. Report failed requests by route/status class with total-attempt denominators,
   timeouts and client delivery failures separately where available. Include
   incident start/end, impact, recovery evidence and unresolved data integrity
   concerns. Unknowns remain unknown; `/healthz` is process liveness only.
7. Attribute cost by provider/service and currency with UTC interval, usage,
   rates, fixed-cost allocation and billing lag. Separate measured usage,
   estimated accrual and finalized charges; state exclusions/credits. Never
   invent a six-hour cost by dividing a monthly bill without an explicit method.
8. A human reviewer verifies the evidence and synthetic exclusion, approves the
   public wording and makes the growth decision against agreed reliability,
   capacity, privacy and spend gates. Record owner and next review time. Public
   publication needs separate authorization; this task prohibits external posts.

If historical evidence is irrecoverable, publish that limitation only after human
review and keep this acceptance criterion unmet. A future measured six-hour run
must be identified as a new observation, not backdated to launch. Any telemetry
change needs a separate privacy-reviewed implementation; none is introduced here.

## Risks and preserved controls

The app remains a single-writer JSON persistence system; independent replicas can
split state. Readiness workload numbers are planning assumptions, not load-test
evidence. Historical alert/readiness documents contain superseded source/target
statements; use the later release history in `docs/launch-merge-policy.md` and
verify the actual window rather than deploying to resolve a documentation gap.
No uptime, notification delivery, recovery, cost ceiling or production override
has been verified by this review.

Existing source policy is preserved: quorum **10**, strict yes majority,
**24-hour exposure**, **one current-head approval**, empty base/check allowlists
(fail closed), `enabled=false`, `dryRun=true`, 09:00 America/Chicago and maximum
six-hour recovery. Protected-base evidence, clean mergeability, current-head
checks, review constraints and sensitive-change maintainer approval remain
required. `PR_AUTO_MERGE_ENABLED=false` is an additional code lock; nonlegacy PR
scopes remain disabled/dry-run and issues never merge. Advisory votes confer no
verified merge authority. Source defaults do not establish production settings.

Required main checks remain `bun test`, `bun run typecheck`, and `route smoke
test`, with at least one human approval and current-revision review. Remote
protection activation was not verified. Production requires a human-published
tagged release; automatic deployment remains operator gated. No quorum, auth,
checks, roster, source identity, vote, receipt, record or environment was changed.
No production access/write, deployment, merge, release or external message was
performed. Analysis PRs/commits must use the plain `Refs:` reference above and
leave Linear open; administrator review is required if linked analysis merges
would otherwise auto-close it.

These results validate source contracts, not production adoption or acceptance.
