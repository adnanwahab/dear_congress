## Proposed pilot contract

Start with one consenting locality, a bounded time window and a small explicitly
opted-in corpus. Run offline/shadow clustering; new clusters are pending private
human review. Do not feed pilot counts into demand, ballots, synthesis weights,
repository merge eligibility or official-government claims. A moderator must
approve a cluster revision and each public exemplar before publication.

1. Snapshot eligible source records and their revisions without overwriting text,
   tags or source identifiers. Keep source repository/client identity and item
   kind in references; never treat an answer, proposal or vote as a journal.
   Preserve source classifications, but do not treat client-supplied source or
   synthetic flags as verified provenance. Exclude missing-consent, pending,
   hidden, deleted, synthetic and seeded entries from the real pilot corpus.
2. Partition by a versioned locality mapping, fixed half-open UTC time windows
   and topic taxonomy. Missing location goes to an explicit unknown bucket,
   rather than inferring residence from wording. Nearby cells and window edges
   need explicit review; coordinates alone do not establish jurisdiction.
   Public locality summaries must respect existing suppression/privacy controls.
3. Generate bounded similarity candidates inside each partition, score with
   pinned features/model and abstain on ambiguity. Negation, different desired
   outcomes and context must be represented. Avoid single-link transitive chains
   joining mutually incompatible endpoints. Multi-topic entries may have separate
   memberships with an explicit count denominator; never silently duplicate
   distinct-participant totals across a combined view.
4. Store membership as a derived sidecar: entry ID/revision, cluster revision,
   reason codes, matched evidence spans, uncertainty label, exclusions and run ID.
   Keep private evidence private. Public explanations may summarize exclusions
   by category only when safe; they must not reveal excluded wording, author IDs,
   rare sensitive attributes or identifiers through counts/differences.
5. Show entry count separately from an optional credible distinct-participant
   count. Anonymous cookies and journal-owner hashes identify browsers, not
   people. With current data, label the person count unavailable. Future consented
   verification may use private scoped deduplication keys; publish only suppressed
   aggregates with method, coverage and limitations. Repeated text, names, IPs or
   advisory votes are not verified identity evidence.
6. Maintain pending/approved/rejected/superseded cluster revisions. Record a human
   decision, reason, membership revision and before/after IDs for merges/splits.
   Reject stale review submissions; replay prior history rather than rewriting
   it. Cluster operations must leave journal text, existing need IDs, allocations,
   ballots, proposal revisions and merge receipts untouched.

For replay, manifest the source snapshot digest, schema, consent/visibility
snapshot, model weights/artifact digest, tokenizer/normalization, taxonomy,
geography mapping, thresholds, seed, algorithm and runtime versions. Pin sort and
tie-breaking rules; canonicalize records by stable source/revision IDs and output
serialization. Store model outputs if the provider cannot reproduce them; a model
name alone is insufficient. Test reordered input, worker counts and retries.
Identical eligible snapshot plus manifest must produce identical membership and
explanation digests. Human decisions are a separately versioned input to a
published revision. Withdrawal changes eligibility and creates a new snapshot;
remove withdrawn public exemplars and invalidate cached derivatives without
republishing withdrawn data to demonstrate historical reproducibility.

## Product walkthrough to implement and verify

On `/needs`, a pilot cluster card shows locality/window/topic, advisory AI label,
human-review state, entry count, uncertainty and the participant-count limitation.
An authorized exemplar uses an approved public excerpt; the original wording stays
attached to its protected source record. Default to no public exemplars until
authorization exists. Do not expose display names or automatically publish raw
journal snippets; redaction alone does not establish consent.

Opening “Why grouped?” shows the entry's own matching evidence, cluster revision,
model/run version and safe exclusion categories. “Challenge grouping or tags”
accepts a private explanation, provides a durable receipt, and offers review
status without immediately changing other authors' entries. Retain signed-cookie,
origin, rate-limit and authorization checks. Ownership loss needs a moderated
recovery path, not unrestricted entry-ID editing. A human reviewer can reject,
move or split membership and records a reason; the author sees the outcome and
history. Challenges must survive recomputation and stale revisions must not
overwrite newer decisions. Journal abuse reports are a separate workflow.

Verify keyboard-only use, visible focus, labeled controls, screen-reader reading
order, status announcements, error recovery and focus return after a challenge.
Uncertainty/review state cannot rely on color. Check narrow screens, zoom and
long original text; private evidence must be absent from HTML, JSON, SSE and
accessibility text. No product walkthrough or accessibility pass is claimed here.

## Fixtures and million-item benchmark specification

Implement a streaming generator and offline runner using disposable data only,
with a fixed PRNG, seed, version and expected source digest. Generate exactly
1,000,000 synthetic journals with ground-truth locality/window/topic and intended
group IDs; do not commit a million-line corpus or ingest it into production.
Use a small committed representative subset plus generator parameters.

Include paraphrases and exact duplicates; repeated submissions by one participant;
identical wording from different participants; transit versus school buses;
rent increase versus desire to increase rent; negation, sarcasm and ambiguous
“safety”; multilingual/Unicode text; multi-topic needs; boundary timestamps and
locations; missing/invalid metadata; very large hot partitions; sparse partitions;
PII, withdrawn consent, hidden/moderated entries, synthetic-source spoofing and
concurrent challenges. Include the A~B, B~C, A!~C chaining case. Identity and
privacy fixtures must have explicit expected inclusion and public projection.

Report hardware/OS/Bun versions, code/manifest/input digests, item count, model
costs and whether embedding time is included, concurrency, warm-up policy,
elapsed time, entries/second, p50/p95/p99 assignment latency and peak RSS.
Separate generation, preprocessing, candidate search, scoring, review-queue
creation and end-to-end timings. Include skew tests and bounded memory/candidate
counts rather than an all-pairs million-item comparison. Report repeated runs and
dispersion; synthetic throughput is not production capacity evidence.

Measure false merges as incompatible ground-truth pairs among sampled co-clustered
pairs. Publish numerator/denominator, deterministic sample seed/size, sampling
method and uncertainty interval, stratified by ambiguity, language and geography.
Also sample missed related pairs and abstentions, report review workload and
manually adjudicate a blinded sample. Exact duplicates alone cannot measure
semantic quality. No throughput, latency, false-merge rate or fixture completion
is claimed by this report. Human pilot owners must set quality/privacy stop
criteria before interpreting benchmark results as permission to scale.
