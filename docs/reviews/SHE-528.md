## Required evidence contract before a replay card

Extend the existing typed citizen-proposal API and additive persistence after the
SHE-305/SHE-307 publication and reconsideration foundations exist. Do not create a
parallel local-storage ledger or backfill causal events from current state.

1. Bind every event to the same repository/source identity, citizen item kind and
   proposal ID. Preserve existing source journal links. Public revision records
   must retain exact immutable before/after text and revision IDs.
2. Cite the stored objection by stable locator and exact text/version. A recorded,
   authorized proposer response must explicitly link that objection and the
   resulting revision. Temporal proximity or matching words is insufficient.
   Existing anonymous publishing-browser authority must not be called verified
   resident identity. Never expose ownership or voter hashes.
3. Require a real nonempty text change, compare the referenced exact revisions,
   and retain the record's chronology. Introduce stable response/event IDs and a
   sequence order for timestamp ties; do not guess ordering from array joins.
4. Require an explicit advisory reconsideration recorded against that resulting
   revision after publication. Preserve prior ballots and their revision scope;
   never transfer support or reconstruct historic choices from today's totals.
   Unchanged support, unsure, withdrawal and continued opposition remain distinct.
5. Project a card only when all four linked records exist, their scopes agree,
   and chronology is valid. Missing, stale, malformed, cross-source, unpublished
   or inaccessible records yield no completed influence card. Never fetch private
   drafts to fill gaps. Partial public history can remain in its ordinary ledger.

The public copy should say “Recorded response and revision,” and explicitly state:
“A revision or compromise does not mean the objector endorses the proposal.
Reconsideration is advisory.” Neither an accepted objection nor animation implies
agreement, representative support, legislative enactment or merge authority.
Keep real citizen activity, fictional sample activity and model output in separate
labelled views with separate identities; no synthetic completed chain on real cards.

## Storyboard and validation plan

Keep a chronological static changelog with source anchors, recorded timestamps,
response wording, exact diff and revision-bound advisory event. It must expose all
the information without playback. Add an opt-in replay with native Previous,
Next, Play/Pause and Restart buttons; no autoplay or hover-only content. Reuse the
existing diff's Removed/Added labels, escaping and whitespace preservation, and
the shared motion scale. Reduced motion or motion off skips animated movement;
manual stepping and static history remain usable. Stop timers on pause, unmount,
source change and motion-preference changes. Playback never submits a vote.

Required fixtures and interaction evidence before acceptance:

| State | Expected result |
| --- | --- |
| Complete linked sequence | Four ordered stages, exact citations and diff; static and replay content agree. |
| Missing any stage; unchanged text; mismatched revision/source/kind | No completed card and no inferred influence. |
| Declined/reopened objection; continued opposition | Preserve recorded wording; no endorsement or success framing. |
| Loading, empty, failed request, missing record | Distinct status/error/empty copy; retry reads only; no fabricated fallback. Abort or ignore stale responses after navigation. |
| Privacy and persistence | No private fields in projection; old records/ballots unchanged; additive migration, restart, concurrent-write and rollback checks. |
| Keyboard and touch | All controls operable without hover/drag; visible focus; source links reachable; no focus theft on timed steps; narrow-screen diff reflows. |
| Reduced motion, motion off, pause and restart | No automatic movement under reduced motion; content and chronology unchanged; no writes or extra requests during playback. |

Run model/API isolation tests and rendered DOM tests plus native desktop/mobile,
keyboard-only, touch, reduced-motion, zoom/forced-colors and screen-reader journeys
on disposable local data. Do not claim these future journeys passed from this
analysis or from the pre-existing unrelated route screenshots.
