# SHE-493 — interactive illustrative fiscal receipt

Refs: https://linear.app/shel-bernstein/issue/SHE-493/joystory-where-a-budget-dollar-goes-interactive-receipt

## Implementation

The existing PlanBudget in the synthesis stage now includes a proposal-level
receipt in both real-input and blended-input lanes. Native labeled selects switch
between whole-proposal dollars per year and dollars per resident per year. Typed
conversion contracts consume the current ledger rows, including local revisions,
without changing proposal IDs, source selections, votes or server state. The
receipt does not aggregate different budget levels under one population count.

Amounts remain simulated model assumptions even in the real-input lane. The
receipt prominently says it is not a verified personal tax bill. It requests no
personal financial information and sends/saves nothing. It shows budget level,
jurisdiction, amount provenance, missing estimate date and uncertainty, annual
model period (no fiscal year specified), and unspecified funding source. It does
not infer a revenue mix from a proposal's implementing agency or mechanism.
One-time capital costs remain an explicitly disclosed model limitation.

## Sources and arithmetic

Frozen April 1, 2020 Census population counts, verified against Census Bureau
sources during this task on November 14, 2026:

- United States: 331,449,281 (50 states and DC, excluding territories), and Texas:
  29,145,505 — [2020 apportionment table 2](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/2020/data/apportionment/apportionment-2020-table02.pdf).
- Houston: 2,304,580 — [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/houstoncitytexas/POP010220).
- Harris County: 4,731,145 — [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/harriscountytexas/POP010220).

These historical resident counts include children and non-taxpayers and are not
current estimates or beneficiary counts. Each receipt exposes its exact count,
scope, source link and date. USD millions × 1,000,000 ÷ matching residents ÷ one
model year gives the illustrative annual equal share. Annual costs are already
annualized; no arbitrary multi-year denominator is invented. Values round only
for display; positive sub-cent results say less than $0.01. Nonfinite, negative,
overprecision or unsafe converted amounts fail closed. Unsupported jurisdictions
show unavailable population while annual view remains usable.
