## Proposed delivery contract

The following specifies future behavior, not available APIs.

1. Introduce a need-board identity with repository identity, source kind/ID,
   stable jurisdiction ID and explicitly published board revision. Jurisdiction
   label, authority evidence and need/topic ID are separate fields. Link exactly
   two distinct jurisdiction boards by reviewed IDs; reject missing, inaccessible,
   withdrawn, duplicate or stale sources. Comparable concern is a deliberate
   participant/moderator selection, never inferred from personal location or prose.
   Do not backfill legacy records with guessed residency or sharing consent.
2. A bridge references both boards without merging their records. Show two
   clearly named region panels with separate evidence, sources, proposed actions,
   authority limitations and dates. Put region labels on messages and shared
   artifacts, while marking self-described locality unverified. No combined
   electorate, pooled votes, regional mandate, residency verification or legal
   authority is created by joining. Original proposal votes, objections and
   journal links remain independent. Unknown authority stays unknown.
3. Persist rooms, memberships, expiring invitations, scoped moderator assignments,
   artifact grants, messages, reports and minimal audit events in new namespaces.
   Use the repository's serialized single-writer persistence with atomic commit,
   rollback and restart tests. Do not reuse temporary live-round maps for durable
   discussion/audit. Keep old sample-round endpoints and ballots unchanged.
4. Default newly created room material to Personal. Distinguish Public,
   Invite-only and Personal in text at entry, heading, composer and sharing review.
   Personal is owner-only; Invite-only requires active admitted membership;
   Public exposes only explicitly approved artifacts. A public room must not
   expose a linked private artifact, roster, report or invitation. Copied URLs,
   presenter flags and guessed IDs confer no role. Check current authorization
   on list/detail/search/export/source-preview and every stream emission.
5. Show exact content, original source/revision, region, intended audience and
   retention limits before each share. Bind consent to the artifact revision and
   audience; edits or wider audience changes invalidate consent. Board linking
   never republishes every journal, comment, private draft or source attachment.
   Publication of an original record is not blanket consent for bridge curation.
   Moderators cannot publish another person's personal material by approving it.
6. Allow leave, owner revocation and reporting as distinct actions. Leaving ends
   membership and closes its streams; grant revocation removes future access and
   excludes the artifact from bridge projections. Neither silently deletes an
   independent public proposal or modifies its ballots. Clear inaccessible client
   content on denial. Explain that recipients' previously downloaded copies cannot
   be recalled. Define content and audit retention separately before enrollment;
   do not promise automatic deletion unsupported by backup/operator procedures.
7. Messages require explicit submission and intended audience. Define pending,
   approved, rejected and withdrawn states, assigned moderator powers, reasoned
   decisions and a private report/receipt path. Reports must target a typed room
   or artifact revision, not masquerade as journal entry reports. Pending content
   and reporter identity never enter public payloads or audit views. Keep escaped
   text rendering, body/rate limits, same-origin protection and server-only secrets.
8. Require expected room, membership/grant and source revisions on mutations.
   Deny expired/revoked sessions before writes or fanout; stale operations return
   conflict without mutation. Idempotency reconciles lost successful replies using
   the same operation ID. No automatic publication retry after reconnect. Reject
   old client responses after switching room, source or scope; stop/clear protected
   streams on denial. Reconnect requires a fresh authorized snapshot.
9. Commit successful transitions and minimal audit metadata atomically: opaque
   actor/role, action, room/board/artifact identities, revisions, audience change,
   timestamp and operation ID. Keep raw private text, invite secrets and reporter
   identities out of public audit/history and ordinary logs. A failed save must
   neither broadcast a success nor leave partially updated grants/content.
10. Preserve provenance as structured source data. Fixture boards and demo
    messages carry conspicuous fictional labels at entry and per artifact.
    Synthetic model actors, illustrative policy support and SSE connections never
    become real contributors or attendee counts. Empty real rooms stay empty.

Integration: CitizenProposals should offer an explicit source-bound bridge entry
after valid board binding and consent review. LiveRound should link a separate
bridge mode/component, preserving sample-only advisory rounds. TopicHubs may
project only currently authorized, explicitly public bridge artifacts; retain
existing exclusion of human stories without curation consent. Update public
participation/privacy guidance only when the corresponding behavior exists.
