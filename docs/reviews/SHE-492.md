Refs: https://linear.app/shel-bernstein/issue/SHE-492/joystory-government-responsibility-explorer-as-interactive-machine

Implemented a read-only explorer in WorkflowScreen shared by all six stages.
The typed federal and Texas examples show four ordered roles, native expandable
office explanations, source-bearing arrow links and an equivalent text outline.
The layout reflows to two columns and then one column; controls use native
keyboard semantics with explicit focus outlines. No animation is introduced,
so reduced motion has the same static representation. Loading, failure and
missing-selection messages distinguish proposal context from institutional examples.

Sources were inspected on 2026-11-14: USAGov branches and budget process,
Texas Legislative Council legislative process and Texas Constitution Article IV
section 10 and Article VIII section 6. These support institutional roles only.
No claim is made that any sample proposal has confirmed legal authority or funding.
City/county/transit overlap is explicitly unresolved, rather than inferred from
fictional sponsor categories. Proposal identifiers, sources and revisions are not
used as authority records; the component reads title/jurisdiction only and writes
no storage, vote, outreach, production or governance data.
