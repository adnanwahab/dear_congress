`docs/launch-merge-policy.md`. Existing stats use a separate durable SQLite store
and authenticated, script-free `/admin/stats`. Hero navigation leads to sample
WorkflowScreen voting, ArgumentMap expansion and AdvisoryShare preview; choice

for `repo:adnanwahab/dear_congress SHE-855` and
`repo:adnanwahab/dear_congress is:open analytics` returned no PRs on November 16,
2026. This is a bounded search, not evidence that every related PR was reviewed.

Six event names and exact trigger/coverage definitions are in README's Aggregate

exclude identifiers, content, stance and session tracking, and cannot submit
choice-save/withdrawal or landing event names. Choice counts come from successful
server responses, including repeated saves and removal of already absent choices.
No first-session, unique-person, comprehension or causal conversion claim is made.
Sample proposal displays/arguments/shares and broader civic/citizen choice actions
are explicitly distinguished in dashboard wording, so they are not a cohort funnel.
Self-selection, bot inflation, underdelivery and share/participation limits are
visible in the dashboard. No fixture tally is added to these counts.
