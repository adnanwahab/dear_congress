## Behavior and limits

Opening the optional household demo on /synth exposes a paired balance replay.
Checking the credit changes only $0 to $150/month and starts three monthly frames.
Baseline and intervention stay side by side; changed balances receive a dashed
border, explicit dollar difference and a short reveal animation. Reduced-motion
CSS removes the animation. Pause, Replay, Next month and a labeled month slider
allow manual inspection without depending on animation. Household selection
remounts the replay and clears its prior state; leaving the demo clears timers.
The dated month-close log references generated credit event IDs. January through
April 2030 dates are explicitly fictional scenario labels, not measurements,
official schedules or implementation promises. The full existing source ledger
and its event IDs remain available below the replay.

The demo discloses omitted funding, eligibility and behavioral responses and
unknown real effects. No uncertainty intervals or calibrated causal outcomes
are claimed. No selected-package, external world, source-backed measurement or
historical-outcome integration is implemented. Source/safety/editorial and manual
browser/assistive-technology review remain required before broader acceptance.
