such as “Where would you like to start?” and equally prominent ordinary links:

- **Explore a proposal & advisory vote** → `/votes`. State beside the link that
  these are fictional sample proposals; this destination offers a ballot in one
  navigation without inventing a proposal ID or using the repository electorate.
- **Tell us what needs fixing** → `/journal`. This opens the existing write tab;
  publishing remains a separate explicit action.

Suggested two-sentence guidance:

> No account is needed: participation is advisory, not an official election or
> government decision, and sample proposals and counts are illustrative.
> Journal entries you publish are shared publicly, so leave out names, addresses,
> contact details and other private information.

Move the six existing steps into a later native `<details>` with a “How it works”
`<summary>`, collapsed initially; keep their content, destinations and the rest of
the homepage below. Correct the account wording without changing ballot rules.
Keep the start panel independent of `/api/demo` so both links work when that
request fails. Do not introduce first-run storage, identity checks, automatic
publishing, votes or an inference workflow for this small entry-point change.
