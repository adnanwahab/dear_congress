## Behavior

Selecting a need now previews cells containing that need, even when another need
has more mentions there. Markers and list counts remain source-specific in Both
mode: green submitted accounts and outlined ochre simulated estimates. Markers
are positioned within their aggregate grid, never at household coordinates.
Measured conditions retain their separate sourced panel; they are not invented
as neighborhood measurements or conflated with account counts. Existing server
0.01-degree/minimum-three-real-entry suppression is unchanged.

A bounded camera flight occurs once per selected need with available data.
Refreshing evidence does not reset a user-controlled camera. Explicit cell buttons
zoom to neighborhood context. Retry restores the last map camera. Shared motion
duration and reduced-motion/off preferences apply, with zero-duration movement
when disabled. Stage routes and typed View Transitions remain unchanged.

The same searchable evidence list stays mounted during map loading, resource
failure, timeout and retry. Tile failure preserves the need and list selection.
Native labelled search and buttons provide keyboard/touch operation, with 44px
minimum button height and a bounded scrolling list. Selecting another need clears
stale cell/search selection. Loading, absent evidence and unmatched search have
text states. Stale geographic requests are ignored after mode changes/unmount.
Exploration makes no publication or vote request. Public guide copy is updated.
