## Proposed first-run read-only slice

Add an opt-in **Explore authority and rights** panel on citizen detail and beside
the private amendment comparison. Preserve the current proposed text and provide
an explicit source selection: original proposal or a particular saved amendment.
Never silently analyze unsaved edits or substitute a different available source.

1. Start with “Workshop analysis — draft text is not binding law. Legal
   interpretations require human review.” Show sample/citizen origin, exact text,
   revision, jurisdiction and analysis observation date. No vote or run on entry.
2. Offer a small, repository-authored teaching example, separately labeled
   fictional. For real citizen text without a curated analysis, show “No reviewed
   authority map available” with the original text still readable.
3. Read the text as explicitly selected provisions. Each has linked authority,
   rights constraints, potential governing offices and implementation prerequisites.
   Provide a semantic list/table equivalent to every graph node and edge.
4. Selecting a claim reveals source locator/version, observation time, exact
   relevant excerpt, interpretation, contrary interpretations, missing evidence and
   reviewer status. Selecting an office must not imply its endorsement.
5. Unsupported, disputed or stale provisions stay visibly unresolved. Show
   “No reviewed model mapping; effects unavailable.” Legal review alone must not
   activate a simulation. Existing separate mechanism sandbox remains labeled as
   such and must not inherit the proposed text as a simulated policy.

Do not prefill support, infer public agreement, assign an approval percentage,
invent a fiscal effect or synthesize a pretend source receipt. No new writes,
AI calls, source fetching, or saved review state are needed in the first slice.
Describe an unreviewed map as unreviewed even if a fixture passes structural checks.

## Proposed typed analysis and evidence contract

This is a contract proposal, not an implemented schema:

| Entity | Required binding and fields |
| --- | --- |
| Analysis subject | Exact repository identity, source kind (`sample` or `citizen`), source ID, base snapshot and exact proposed text hash; saved amendment ID when applicable; schema version and analysis revision. Never reuse a GitHub PR number as a civic proposal identity. |
| Provision | Subject revision, explicit text offsets and exact selected text; unknown clause boundaries remain unknown. Reject out-of-range or text-mismatching offsets. |
| Source observation | Canonical publisher/document identity, safe source URL, jurisdiction, instrument kind, locator, source version/effective date if known, fetched/observed timestamp, archived content hash if available, retrieval method and freshness/error status. Unknown dates stay null with an explanation. |
| Interpretation | Provision and source-observation IDs, claim text, author kind (`curated`, `human`, `ai`), generation/method version, limitations, supporting and contrary references. A reference supports a specific claim rather than the whole proposal. |
| Review event | Exact subject/analysis/source versions, authenticated authorized reviewer, scoped decision, rationale and timestamp. Append decisions; supersede explicitly, never overwrite disagreement. AI starts pending human review. |
| Office/prerequisite | Potential office identity and jurisdiction, claim-specific evidence, authority question, required legislative/administrative/funding step and unresolved dependencies. No guessed incumbent, approval, funding or implementation date. |
| Model eligibility | Independent `unavailable` or reviewed mapping revision; precise supported provision, allowlisted parameter/units/bounds and model version. A missing mapping cannot default to ready. |

Edges have explicit types such as `asserts-authority`, `constrains`, `contested-by`,
`potentially-administered-by` and `requires-step`. Validate all referenced nodes
within the same subject revision; reject duplicate IDs, dangling references,
cross-source edges and unsupported schema versions. Cycles may describe contested
reasoning; do not mistake graph traversal for a proof of validity.

Keep review state, source freshness, disputed interpretation and model eligibility
as separate dimensions. A reviewed interpretation may remain contested. A new
source observation or text revision invalidates applicability of prior review;
preserve the old event for its old revision and require an explicit new decision.
Never silently rebase an analysis or carry advisory votes to amended text.

For a future model integration, retain the original validated receipt unchanged
and separately bind it to the exact mapping revision, subject revision and request
identity. Validate model version, seed, parameter values, clock and baseline before
displaying differences. Missing receipt fields remain unavailable. Do not add
fictional provenance fields to an actual provider receipt to make it appear complete.

## Source and human-review work required

Choose a bounded jurisdiction and mechanism before curating actual legal claims.
Collect constitutional text, applicable statutes, local enabling law/charter,
relevant decisions, funding authority and implementing procedures independently.
Federal constitutional text alone cannot populate a city's implementing powers.
Congress.gov metadata alone cannot fill the operative-law source role.

Potential primary research starting points are the official
[Constitution transcription](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVMAN-2020-11-10/pdf/GOVMAN-2020-11-10-Constitution-of-the-United-States-3.pdf)
and the CRS report
[Constitutional Authority Statements and the Powers of Congress](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R44729/R44729.8.pdf),
identified through web search on November 15, 2026. These are research pointers,
not archived application receipts or a reviewed determination about any proposal.
Do not label them current-law verification. No legal applicability conclusion or
statutory coverage claim is established by this exploration.

Each curated example needs a qualified human reviewer to assess applicability,
jurisdiction, effective version, contested interpretation and omissions. Store
review scope explicitly; “reviewed” cannot mean court approval or official
authorization. Do not invent a reviewer identity or present AI confidence as a
probability of constitutionality. Any AI drafting assistance needs separate
disclosure, consent and a privacy contract before transmitting text.

## Interaction, permission and recovery gates

Read-only viewing must have no publication, review, vote or model-run side effects.
Later private annotation must be distinct from public submission and use a new
versioned storage envelope; do not migrate or overwrite existing amendment keys.
Document browser-profile visibility, deletion, storage limits and lack of recovery.
Do not place private text, author names, locations or citation notes in URLs/logs.

Public review writes need authenticated role checks at the server, exact expected
revision, same-origin/CSRF protections, bounded input, rate limits, idempotent
retries and durable append history. Existing anonymous civic cookies do not confer
review authority; verified repository membership also does not establish legal
expertise. Return conflict on stale revisions and retain the working annotation.

Absent sources show unavailable, not withdrawn or legally invalid. Failed refresh
retains clearly stale last-good evidence without advancing its timestamp. Unknown
source versions and malformed/future dates block a fresh/reviewed presentation.
Switching sources cancels/ignores late responses. Retry explicitly without changing
subject, casting a response, or regenerating a successful receipt. Escape all text;
validate outbound links and never fetch arbitrary submitted URLs server-side.

Every significant displayed quantity needs units, origin, observed/run time,
method and applicable uncertainty/limitations beside it. Counts must describe
their denominator and exclusions; an analysis-node count is not a legal score.
Synthetic metrics retain model version, seed, population, recorded clock and
limitations. Unknown cost/effect is unavailable rather than zero. No real-world
effect estimate or public-consensus percentage is proposed in the initial slice.
