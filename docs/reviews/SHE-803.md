## Change

A first-run action above the map metrics, **Run modeled county evaluation**,
immediately improves and evaluates one sample-plan revision using the existing
algorithm. It stops automatic playback and leaves a stable result for inspection.
The status announces 3,144 evaluated synthetic scenarios, revision and result;
county colors, tooltips, priorities and selected-county explanation update together.
Restart restores the unevaluated state and first-run action. Existing continuous
simulation remains optional.

Initial agreement metrics, methodology and accessible map description now say
Not evaluated rather than displaying an agreement fraction or percentage. The
initial support progress bar is absent and its caption says no result exists.
Geographic coverage and evaluated-count metrics remain separate. The legend's
zero agreeing models is explicitly scoped alongside 3,144 Not evaluated models.
Public guide explains the action and local-only synthetic limits.

All FIPS identities, model thresholds, equal weighting and plan-revision behavior
remain intact. No APIs, storage, votes, PR binding, records or receipts changed.
This independent advisory beta records no resident ballots in the map and makes
no eligibility, representative participation or official-ballot claims.
