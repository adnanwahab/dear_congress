checkpoint. No interpolation, probability interpretation or cross-unit ranking.

selects and disclosures provide keyboard/touch controls. Input changes unmount
and abort the experiment, removing previous results.

major uncertainty, workload caps, untested assumptions and full deterministic
receipts. Replays pin the previous model version. Mismatched request identities,
versions, horizon endpoints, clocks, engines, checkpoint grids and baseline
