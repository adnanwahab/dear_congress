# SHE-974 — Launch pilot: first 15 minutes of an incident

Refs: https://linear.app/shel-bernstein/issue/SHE-974/cs-l100085-write-a-15-minute-incident-playbook-for-the-launch-pilot

Status: review candidate; documentation and agent tabletop only. No live controls,
owner acceptance, production rehearsal or acceptance signoff established. Keep the
issue open for human verification. Scope: adnanwahab/dear_congress, an independent
advisory beta, not election infrastructure. Prioritize observed harm and actual
pilot traffic, not aspirational audience size.

## Before a promotion window

The human launch maintainer must record: dated window and timezone, accepted
incident commander (proposed: Adnan; acceptance pending), accepted backup,
operator with hosting access, privacy reviewer, communications owner and an agreed
private escalation channel. One human may cover several roles; record this
explicitly. Commander acknowledges within five minutes; otherwise escalate to the
accepted backup. Without accepted coverage and tested containment, postpone
promotion. Agents do not assume these human roles.

Record confirmed origin, Fly app, current release tag/full SHA/image digest,
previous human-published tagged release and immutable image digest, single writer
and volume identity, protected backup reference, and the approved containment and
recovery procedure. Do not paste credentials or database contents. Current source
fly.toml targets civic-synthesis; confirm live identity rather than relying on
older launch-alerts.md target and instrumentation statements. Follow
[alert ownership and evidence](../launch-alerts.md),
[release parity](../release-parity.md), and
[merge/release controls](../launch-merge-policy.md).

## Severity and decision owner

| Level | Evidence or trigger | Human decision and immediate response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEV1 critical | Suspected privacy exposure, unauthorized mutation, lost/misattributed votes, wrong repository or PR revision receiving ballots, or unsafe content that cannot be contained | Commander activates operator containment immediately; privacy reviewer joins. Treat uncertain vote integrity as critical pending investigation. |
| SEV2 major | Voting unavailable, sustained failures, coordinated abuse, or misleading civic/source claims spreading during promotion, without evidence of exposure or corruption | Commander pauses promotion and affected writes; escalate to SEV1 if scope is unclear or containment fails. |
| SEV3 limited | Isolated UI/copy fault or a single report with bounded impact and intact records | Commander assigns review, preserves evidence, and monitors; escalate on repeated reports or wider harm. |

Disagreement or political stance alone is not abuse. Review conduct and source
provenance consistently. Never fabricate participation, imply official endorsement,
or solicit unconsented outreach. Do not repeat harmful text in public notes.

## Minute-by-minute response card

Time starts at first credible report. These are deadlines, not a reason to delay
containment. Every step records UTC time, human actor, decision and verification.
If not contained by minute 15, keep containment active and continue response.

| Elapsed | Human owner | Action and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2 min | First responder → commander | Open a restricted incident record; assign severity, affected routes/repository/item kind/PR head and base, symptom and first-known time. Pause planned promotion through its human owner. Capture redacted status, release identity and request IDs; do not reproduce private payloads. |
| 2–5 min | Hosting operator, directed by commander | Stop affected writes using the pretested hosting containment procedure below. Pause background jobs, internal workers and external webhook delivery as applicable; disabling buttons is insufficient. Keep governance disabled. Escalate immediately if access or coverage is missing. |
| 5–8 min | Operator + communications owner | Serve the approved static incident notice or read-only banner through the pretested hosting path. Verify the public origin and any direct origin cannot bypass containment. Record what is actually available; use “temporarily unavailable” if reads are also blocked. |
| 8–12 min | Release owner + privacy reviewer | Assess release regression vs abuse vs exposure. Preserve protected backup and revision/receipt evidence. Decide whether rollback is safe under the rules below; do not overwrite data. Restrict exposed reads immediately, even if that means full unavailability. |
| 12–15 min | Commander + communications owner | Review the draft public note, choose next update time (within 30 minutes), and assign follow-up owners. Record rollback/hold decision and unresolved scope. Human publication is separate from this document; keep promotion paused until explicit go decision. |

## Stop writes and show the notice: actual capability limits

Audited server.ts and src/server/hbl: there is **no global read-only flag or incident
banner** in this revision. ADMIN_TOKEN controls selected admin actions, not all
public writes. HBL_KILL_SWITCH governs merge work, not civic voting, journals,
proposal discussion, live rounds, OAuth/sync or every external side effect.
Some governance GET handlers sync and write caches/identity state. A blanket
POST/PATCH/PUT/DELETE block alone cannot establish storage read-only mode.

A human operator must first qualify a reversible hosting-level procedure in an
isolated environment: route the public hostname to a static maintenance page,
block direct access to the application origin, and quiesce the application and
all writers/worker schedules without destroying machines, volumes or records.
Keep auto-start/routing from reviving a writer. Record exact provider steps,
previous routing/worker settings and restoration steps in the restricted operator
record. No such edge/static service or operational command is verified here;
do not invent a Fly switch or execute an untested command under pressure.

If a tested selective gate exists, it must reject all mutations before handlers,
cover API aliases, admin, webhooks, internal workers and direct origin, and allow
only proven non-mutating reads. Otherwise use full application isolation and a
static notice. If that is unavailable, the operator must isolate service access
and accept downtime; do not label an operating app read-only. Verify rejection
and unchanged stores in staging, not by casting production test votes. During an
incident use routing/process evidence and safe public reads; /healthz only proves
liveness, never write safety. Alert observers that maintenance may fire monitors.

Approved draft copy, after human review:

> Civic Synthesis participation is temporarily paused while we investigate a
> reported issue. New submissions and vote changes are unavailable. This is an
> independent advisory beta, not an official voting service. Next update:
> [UTC time].

Only add “Existing pages remain available read-only” after verifying that claim.
Do not claim records are intact until integrity checks support it. A banner alone
is not containment. Maintain a timestamped fallback note on a human-approved
status surface if the application is unavailable.

## Roll back a release without rolling back people's records

The human release owner records the suspect tag/SHA/digest and selects a previous
human-published tagged release by immutable digest, never a mutable latest tag or
an arbitrary main build. Preserve a protected, consistent backup of both JSON
state and governance SQLite (including appropriate SQLite journal/WAL handling).
Keep copies within the approved hosting boundary; no downloads into tickets.
Qualify the previous binary against an isolated restore first: schema compatibility,
cookie/ownership continuity, source IDs, repository/item-kind boundaries, PR
head/base revision binding, ballots, historical records and receipts must survive.
An older binary that cannot safely read current data is not a rollback candidate.

Keep the service contained while the human authorizes and performs the approved
tagged-release recovery. Do not replay votes, reset demo data, restore an old
production snapshot, migrate destructively, or change identity secrets as a
shortcut. A data repair requires a separate human-reviewed recovery plan. If
rollback is unsafe, stay paused and prepare a reviewed forward fix. Agents do not
deploy, publish releases, merge or enable auto-merge for this task.

Before reopening, run required checks on the candidate, verify /version against
its original full SHA using release-parity.md, verify route/security/privacy
behavior, and compare protected record counts and revision bindings. Use isolated
restores for mutation tests. Human commander and operator record go/no-go; privacy
reviewer must concur for exposure incidents. Restore routing and jobs deliberately,
retain governance holds, monitor for recurrence, then resume promotion only by
explicit human decision. Failure returns to containment, not repeated blind deploys.

## Privacy, abuse and misinformation response

Restrict public access to affected content first. The authenticated moderation
surface (/admin/moderation, /api/admin/moderation) handles journal review only;
do not assume it moderates every proposal/comment surface. Use it only within
human authority and supported scope. For broader exposure, isolate affected reads
or the whole app. Preserve minimal restricted evidence: entry/source IDs, times,
affected surfaces, access scope, reviewer and actions. Avoid full journal text,
IP addresses, contact details, cookies, tokens and raw log dumps in public issues.

/privacy offers same-browser journal deletion and a public GitHub removal-request
fallback with entry link/ID only; no private contact channel is published. The
privacy reviewer must establish a restricted channel before requesting sensitive
details. Do not invite a person to repost exposed content publicly. Deletion of a
journal does not erase independently published proposals, ballots, historical
repository records, backups or third-party copies. Review each affected surface
and document retention/removal limits; never promise complete erasure. Record who
assesses affected-person notifications and applicable obligations without guessing
a legal deadline. Rotate exposed credentials only through authorized operator
response, accounting for identity continuity and service impact.

For misleading claims, retain original provenance and contradictory evidence,
label uncertainty, and prepare a human-reviewed correction. Never silently rewrite
source identity or counts. Apply conduct-based abuse review without stance-based
targeting; do not treat advisory browser votes as verified merge authority.

## Public incident note and restricted incident log

Draft for the communications owner to review and publish on the agreed surface:

> [UTC] We are investigating [verified symptom] affecting [bounded surface] since
> [time, or “start time under investigation”]. We have [verified containment].
> [Confirmed impact; otherwise “Impact on stored records is still being checked.”]
> Please wait before retrying submissions. Civic Synthesis is an independent
> advisory beta. Next update: [UTC within 30 minutes].

Do not publish suspect identities, private content, guessed cause, unverified
participation or assurances of no loss. Update on schedule even if investigation
continues. Resolution note states observed impact, recovery time, verified checks,
remaining uncertainty and follow-up owner; human approval and publication remain
required. No external messages are sent by this task.

Restricted record fields: incident ID; detection/acknowledgment times; severity;
accepted commander/backup/operator/privacy/comms owners; channel; origin and
release identity; affected source/repository/kind/head/base; containment settings
and verification; protected backup reference; impact and uncertainty; rollback or
hold decision with human authorization; public-note approvals/URLs and next update;
reopening checks/decision; follow-up owners and dated handoff. Public issue stores
only redacted pointers and non-sensitive conclusions.

## Preserved merge policy

src/server/hbl/pilot.ts hard-disables PR auto merge. Source defaults remain disabled,
dry-run, quorum 10, 24-hour exposure, one current-head approval, empty base/check
allowlists (fail closed), 09:00 America/Chicago, recovery at most six hours, and an
active-by-default kill switch. Other repository scopes remain disabled/dry-run;
issues never merge. Preserve strict yes majority, protected-base evidence, clean
mergeability, current-head checks, blocking-review restrictions and sensitive-change
human approval. Do not reduce quorum, checks or auth during recovery. Production
environment values and GitHub branch protection have not been verified here.

Required main checks: bun test, bun run typecheck, route smoke test; at least one
human approval and latest-revision review are required by policy. Files do not
activate hosted protection. Release workflow remains human-published non-prerelease
v* tags, validated SHA on main, with PRODUCTION_RELEASES_ENABLED operator hold.
Do not enable that hold or weaken any stricter live settings. Follow the linked
launch merge policy; a merged document is not acceptance.
