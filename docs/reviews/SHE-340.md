# SHE-340 — Phone demo surface

Refs: https://linear.app/shel-bernstein/issue/SHE-340/demo-make-the-phone-experience-demo-ready

Implemented the phone navigation in Navigation.tsx/styles.css. At <=600px the
purple bar has six equal columns: Published, Feed, Votes, Draft, Sponsors, More.
Outcomes, Map, Journal and every existing utility link remain in a native modal
dialog. The nominal bar height is 61px (52px tiles, 8px padding, 1px border), plus
the bottom safe area. The shell reserves 64px plus that safe area. Desktop keeps
all destinations and utility links. No source identities or links were replaced.

The sheet has a labelled heading, focused Close button, native modal focus
containment/return and Esc behavior, explicit outside-click dismissal, an expanded
state on More, and scrollable content. Crossing to desktop closes the sheet.
The workflow header sticks at the top with a compact brand and current-stage pill.

Touch pointer gestures on workflow content advance/reverse through all six stages
at a 60px horizontal threshold and 2:1 horizontal intent. Vertical intent,
pointercancel and multiple pointers cancel navigation. Controls, links, editable
content, canvas/maps and horizontally overflowing scroll containers are excluded.
Selected proposal IDs are read at navigation time and encoded through stageRoute.
Normal cross-document navigation reuses SHE-323 direction and reduced-motion
behavior. Maps on their separate routes have no stage gesture listener surface.
Phone width rules constrain media, inputs and flex/grid children; tables and code
retain local horizontal scrolling. Workflow pages with tables opt out of the
ancestor pan-y restriction so native sideways table gestures remain available.
