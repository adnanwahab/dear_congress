  temporary 2021 credit rules, not a poverty effect.

  it is not an outcome evaluation or current tax advice.

  on the historical release. Do not silently remove that warning or present the
  historical numbers as the latest estimates.

Editorial inference: a national before/after movement alone cannot identify the
credit's causal effect. Employment and simultaneous programs are competing
explanations, not quantified adjustments in this case. We do not reproduce the
source's benefit-removal estimates as experimental causal estimates. There is no
claim that the website, any local proposal or simulated outreach caused change.
Survey uncertainty remains; no interval or promise of persistent effects is invented.
