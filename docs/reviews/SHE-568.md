## Proposed read-only projection contract (not implemented)

Use namespaced node identities containing source repository, item kind and
source ID; include immutable revision/head/base where applicable. Keep public
node summaries separate from raw journals, ownership hashes, coordinates,
moderation metadata and ballot identities. Public status alone must not qualify
a human story for curation: require an explicit, revocable review/consent record
with scope and date. Recheck eligibility on every projection read and invalidate
cached detail after withdrawal/hiding. A seeded story may demonstrate mechanics
only with a permanent fictional label; it cannot meet consented-human acceptance.

Each edge needs source/target identities, a typed relation, provenance kind
(stored extraction, catalog rule, explicit citation, revision record or model
receipt), evidence locator/version and availability. Validate both endpoints and
revision compatibility before emitting it. Reject missing/unsafe evidence,
cross-repository collisions, stale heads and unsupported model mappings. A
background citation edge means “cites,” never “enacted,” “derived from,” or
“caused.” Missing evidence suppresses the edge rather than generating an example.

Use separate visual/text legends for public participation, fictional repository
samples, synthetic model runs and measured observations. Every total must carry
its unit, inclusion rules, time window and denominator; unknown denominators say
unknown. Browser identities are not people. Model population/runs are not
participants; source observations are not votes. No particle count or size may
imply unrecorded popularity or millions of computed futures.

## Implementation increments and release gates

1. Add the projection and negative contract tests first. Exercise missing consent,
   hidden/withdrawn sources, wrong repository/kind/head, missing evidence, unsafe
   URLs and synthetic-as-measured rejection. Preserve existing stores unchanged.
2. Build static SVG topology plus equivalent semantic links/list on an optional
   route. Reuse actual `/journal?entry=…`, `/needs?need=…&entry=…`,
   `/proposals/:id` and topic/stage links only for their supported identities.
   Do not invent catalog proposal or amendment detail routes. Missing destinations
   remain labeled unavailable. Require route smoke coverage before exposing links.
3. Use stable identity sorting and deterministic positions; keep layout independent
   of votes. Make layer toggles explicit and URL-backed. Start with aggregate
   topic/need nodes and lazy-load bounded detail, never one public person particle.
4. Add reversible camera state and portals through the existing typed motion
   helpers. Preserve selection, layers, camera and return focus in history; plain
   anchors/back must work without View Transitions. Existing stage-direction
   transitions do not themselves establish node-specific focus restoration.
   HBL links may carry only validated public context. A frozen civic snapshot
   and the reviewed-law sandbox must retain their distinct provenance meanings.
5. Gate homepage promotion on native desktop/mobile, keyboard, touch, reduced
   motion, forced colors and manual screen-reader acceptance, plus measured
   rendering/network budgets. Optional WebGL/WebGPU must not be required to read
   or navigate the graph. No homepage promotion is authorized by this review.

Proposed initial budgets to validate on named devices: at most 80 visible nodes,
160 edges, 24 expanded detail items, device-pixel ratio capped at 2, one scheduled
animation frame at a time and no continuous animation while idle/hidden. Use
pagination with a visible truncated/total explanation and abort obsolete detail
requests. Keep initial renderer code lazy and preserve the existing production
shell 250 KB gzip JS check. These are proposed caps, not benchmark results or a
claim of million-record capacity; exercise large synthetic aggregate fixtures
without shipping their invented values as world data.

Use disposable local state and governance disabled. No production writes, public
votes or source imports are needed. This is a script specification for acceptance,
not an executed end-to-end Cosmos demo:

1. Open `/topics/transit`; inspect a returned seeded story's source record and

3. On `/synth`, use reviewed bus law 83, seed 42, population 24; run 100% and

   same baseline, checkpoint and synthetic actor. On provider failure show failure;
   do not fabricate divergence. Full harness instructions are in
   `../live-law-behavior-loop.md`; this review did not execute the HBL provider.
4. Inspect source-linked measured conditions in `/needs`, then
   `/outcomes#policy-measurements`. Distinguish external conditions from fictional
   policy metrics and unestablished causal attribution. Stop at missing policy
   evidence; it is not a completed measured-results loop.

revision, back/focus restoration and no mutation requests. Until then this
walkthrough deliberately exposes disconnected stages and cannot satisfy the
flagship “one concern becomes two worlds and measured results” acceptance.
