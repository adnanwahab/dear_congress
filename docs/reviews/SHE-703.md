## Proposed implementation contract

1. Add a separate optional persisted canvas collection without changing existing
   proposals, comments, votes, journal IDs or repository ballots. Bind each
   artifact to source repository, source kind, source ID and exact source version
   or content digest. Sample, citizen and repository items must remain separate
   even when IDs collide. Never infer authority from a topic or drawing.
2. Use typed agency, obligation, cost, measurement and dependency nodes with
   bounded plain text, finite bounded coordinates, stable node IDs and validated
   edge endpoints. Label all nodes and exports **Participant proposal — not a
   government fact**. Unknown agency, costs and measures remain explicit unknowns.
   Do not prepopulate named attendees or messages. Fixtures must say synthetic
   and remain outside real participation counts.
3. Default to personal scope and show the scope in the editor, listing and export.
   Public means publicly readable, not anonymously writable. Invite-only requires
   explicit per-artifact grants; knowing its ID, parent proposal or stream URL
   grants no access. Personal permits only its owner. Separate view/edit/moderate
   permissions, reject unauthorized HTTP and stream access, and never expose
   browser ownership identifiers or invitation secrets in public payloads.
4. Make invitations revocable and expiring, with hashed secrets and explicit
   recipient redemption. Explain browser-cookie loss and recovery limits before
   granting access. Revoke must terminate access for existing streams and reject
   queued edits, exports and reconnection. Leave removes membership and closes
   the editor; the UI must explain what authored history remains. An owner must
   revoke sharing or resolve ownership before leaving. Scope changes cannot
   silently republish an earlier private revision.
5. Serialize operations with an expected revision and idempotency key. Conflicts
   return a stale-session response and preserve unsent work without applying it
   to another source or revision. Append immutable revisions and audit events
   only after durable save; failed persistence must not broadcast success. Bound
   operation size, node count and history growth. Preserve earlier exports and
   attribution without storing invitation secrets in audit records.
6. Offer explicit sharing for each artifact and reviewed revision, separate from
   workshop attendance. Scope tightening revokes hosted shares immediately.
   Explain that downloaded exports and independent copies cannot be recalled.
   Versioned exports carry schema version, source binding, artifact/revision IDs,
   timestamp, scope and proposal disclaimer. Keep private membership and reports
   out of public exports. Immutable history may retain restricted content, but
   ordinary revision/export APIs must enforce current moderation and access.
7. Provide report controls on artifact and node content, a private bounded report
   queue, authenticated moderator decisions with reasons and audit events, and
   immediate stream invalidation for hidden content. Preserve original records
   under restricted access; do not mutate unrelated source text or ballots.
8. Integrate a canvas entry on the citizen detail, a distinct workshop entry in
   LiveRound without changing round ballots, and opted-in public discovery in
   TopicHubs. Do not copy journal prose, private notes or member lists into a hub.
   Reconcile the CitizenProposals placement with PR #199 before implementing.

## Acceptance verification still required

Use disposable local databases and two independent browser identities. No live
app writes are needed for these checks.

| Area | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Collaboration | Two editors see committed operations; concurrent updates conflict predictably; idempotent retries and restart preserve revisions and exact exports |
| Permission denial | Public reader cannot edit; uninvited identity cannot list/read/stream/export private artifacts; guessed IDs and forged owner fields fail |
| Leave/revoke | Existing streams close and queued writes fail after leave, invitation revocation, scope tightening, expiry and moderation; reconnect cannot regain access |
| Stale sessions | Source/revision changes, slow responses and disconnect/reconnect never overwrite newer work or render content from the previous artifact |
| Privacy | Inspect list/detail/error/stream/export/report payloads and logs for private text, grants and identifiers; sharing one artifact never shares another |
| Moderation | Reports stay private; unauthorized decisions fail; hidden content disappears from active clients, history access and hosted exports while audit remains restricted |
| Provenance | Synthetic attendees/messages are visibly labeled and excluded from contributor counts; every export preserves proposal labeling and source/version binding |
| Accessibility/mobile | Complete creation, editing, connecting, movement, reporting and leaving with keyboard and screen reader; supply a structured text alternative to the drawing; verify focus, live errors, touch controls, narrow viewport, zoom and reduced motion |
