## Evidence and limits

Checked source pages on 2026-11-17:

- Census QuickFacts Chicago and New York: median gross rent, 2020–2024,
  $1,440 and $1,821 per month respectively. Links in the panel resolve to each
  city's QuickFacts page. These pooled survey estimates are not current asking rents.
- CTA fare chart: $2.50 full rail fare from transit account, effective
  2023-02-20, excluding O'Hare premium.
- MTA fare page: $3 regular subway fare for most riders. Effective date was
  not captured and is explicitly unknown. This is not a fare-equivalence claim.
- DOL childcare database: county prices, 2008–2022. No reviewed city-wide
  childcare measure is bundled. Missing data stays missing; no county-to-city
  substitution, annualization, total or eligibility determination occurs.

always shown independently of source check dates. The $100 support example is
explicitly hypothetical and is not bound to any catalog proposal.
