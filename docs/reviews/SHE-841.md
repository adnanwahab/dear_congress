# SHE-841 — consent-based discussion invitations

Refs: https://linear.app/shel-bernstein/issue/SHE-841/cs-l100029-create-a-consent-based-invitation-flow-for-discussion-not

Implemented a separate Feed invitation preview for curated fictional samples.
An explicit device-share or clipboard button exports the exact preview text;
manual selection remains available when APIs are unavailable or denied.
Canceling native share does not copy or send by another route. Switching samples
resets preview and status. The invitation links to Published's reasons/concerns,
retains repository and sample identity, and explains that arguments may be missing,
unreviewed and change over time. No stance, receipt, identity, counts, contact
access, referral query or reward enters this flow. No backend writes were added.
