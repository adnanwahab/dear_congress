A remaining gap was that matching persisted decisions remained usable after
reviewer roles were removed from server configuration. This change requires the
attesting publisher and each of the four expert approvers to retain their
corresponding configured roles. Missing roles fail closed during both readiness
lookup and publisher re-attestation. Credentials and role configuration are not
proof of professional qualifications. Configuration is loaded at startup, so
operator configuration changes take effect on restart. Restoring a role restores
its matching historical receipt unless a later decision has superseded it; no
records or choices are removed.
