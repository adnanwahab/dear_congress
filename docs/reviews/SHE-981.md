## Human-run preview protocol (proposed, not executed)

Before recruitment, name a facilitator and data custodian; provide an approved
private opt-in channel, access-controlled storage, withdrawal contact, exact
retention/deletion dates and a reviewed privacy notice. These are unresolved
operational prerequisites. Do not invent a mailbox or claim guaranteed deletion
from backups. Use an isolated preview with fictional inputs; do not ask testers
to publish journals, vote or submit real personal information on production.

Recruit only through existing explicitly consented research contact lists or
participant-initiated signups. No scraped contacts, cold outreach, political
profiles, inferred affiliations or endorsement claims. Use voluntary self-described
roles solely for recruitment balance. Assign each unique adult participant one
primary cohort so overlapping roles cannot satisfy multiple slots. Target at least
5 educators, 5 public-interest journalists/researchers, and 10 organizers/residents.
Do not enroll students/minors through educators in this initial protocol.

Draft invitation for authorized human use, only after channel details are settled:

> Would you like to opt into a 20-minute preview of Civic Synthesis? This is an
> independent advisory beta, not election infrastructure or an official government
> service. We want feedback on usefulness, clarity and trust. No political views
> are requested. Joining, answering questions and continuing are optional. You may
> skip any task or stop. Before scheduling we will provide who sees your notes,
> their deletion date and how to withdraw. No recording is required.

Obtain separate affirmative permission for participation and note-taking after
providing the completed notice. Recording defaults off; identifiable quotes and
public reuse need separate explicit permission. Declining feedback or recording
must not prevent trying the preview. Do not infer consent from opening a link.

Use the same read-only tasks for every cohort:

1. Open the starting page and explain in your own words what the service does.
2. Find a fictional proposal and its background source; explain what is fictional
   and what the source actually establishes. Do not cast a response.
3. Find participation/privacy guidance and explain whether participation enacts a
   law, verifies a person or permits public reuse of private information.
4. Find the feedback route and explain its public/private limits without submitting.

For each task, record completed independently, completed with help, not completed,
skipped or technical failure, plus elapsed time only with measurement consent.
Define completion as reaching the relevant page and correctly explaining the
requested distinction; facilitator assistance is recorded separately. Stop if a
participant thinks this is an official election service or encounters unexpected
publication; explain the boundary and log a trust blocker without personal text.

Optional feedback: What would you use this for? What seemed unclear or untrustworthy?
What would prevent you using it again? Rate usefulness and confidence in understanding
the service from 1–5, or skip. Never ask political affiliation, voting history,
policy stance, exact address or unnecessary employer/school identity.

## Evidence and acceptance handoff

Keep a private contact/withdrawal mapping separate from pseudonymous session notes.
Suggested restricted ledger fields: random participant ID, one cohort, invitation
permission reference/date, notice version, participation/note consent timestamps,
session date, deployed revision if verified (otherwise unknown), broad device mode,
task statuses, optional durations, opt-in feedback, withdrawal status and deletion
due date. Keep contacts, raw notes and consent receipts out of git and public PRs.
Facilitator deduplicates people through the consented roster, never cookies or IPs.

Public report should contain only reviewed, de-identified aggregate findings.
Report invited, consented, started, finished and feedback-providing counts separately
by cohort, with explicit denominators and skipped/withdrawn counts. Task completion
rate = independent completions / consented task attempts; report assisted results
separately and exclude skips from attempts while reporting them. A page view or
synthetic task run is not a participant. Avoid identifiable small-cell quotes.
This convenience sample cannot establish representativeness or political consensus.

Human acceptance needs dated consent evidence for at least 5/5/10 distinct people,
opt-in feedback and measured completion with missingness disclosed, plus a synthesis
of observed usefulness/trust problems and prioritized follow-up owners. Record
invitation reach and actual participation separately; do not declare the preview
run merely because 20 invitations were sent. Coordinate optional usability reuse
with SHE-982 only under matching consent, retaining separate acceptance criteria.
Do not promote broadly based on a headline count while trust blockers remain.
