## Implementation

An optional native details disclosure, “How to share responsibly,” appears beside
Share preview, independently of preview loading, failure or chosen stance. Its
88-word copy explains the independent non-binding beta, advisory versus official,
synthetic versus verified, original-source/date/version checks, and no endorsement
claims. It preserves private-information and source-context cautions. No consent
checkbox, modal or reading gate is added. The summary has a 44px minimum touch
height and a visible focus outline, with no new animation or request.

The same copy is interpolated into the public guide under a linked heading,
which appears in the existing guide table of contents. Further methodology and
privacy links remain available. This avoids divergent critical copy. No approved
Spanish translation is claimed; existing SHE-318 review requirements still apply.
The SVG exporter, content hash, source identities, optional stance behavior,
repository/item-kind isolation, PR head/base binding, ballots, records and receipts
are unchanged. No production data, external messages or remote mutations occurred.
