## Proposed prototype contract

Start with an isolated fixture-driven prototype and a server aggregation contract,
then validate comprehension and privacy before committing to the full 2–4 week
implementation. A count should carry metric/unit, numerator, denominator (nullable),
population definition, source/origin, collection window, as-of time, geography
definition/version, item identity/revision, inclusion/exclusion rules, missingness,
suppression state and uncertainty status. Preserve exact source repository and
item-kind identity; never pool ballots across proposal texts or PR head/base pairs.

| Metric | Numerator and denominator | Public interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current raw ballots | Accepted active choices for one item/revision and window; no percentage required | Records, not unique people. Separately describe submissions/retries/withdrawals if audited event history exists; do not invent event totals from current state. |
| Distinct unverified browser tokens | Distinct server identities with an active choice in the same scope; denominator is all such active tokens for a choice share | A self-selected browser response share; include unsure, state exclusion of withdrawals and item nonresponse. Cross-item unique counts require an explicit privacy decision. |
| Verified eligible participants | Unavailable until eligibility definition, verification evidence and deduplication policy exist | Show “Not collected,” not zero. Even verified eligibility alone does not establish representative sampling. |
| Observed geography coverage | Counties with qualifying real observations divided by the explicitly supported county universe | Coverage of recorded activity, not population turnout. Geometry-only counties remain “No published observation”; suppression must not expose small-cell presence. |
| Item missingness | Missing required field among records where that field was expected divided by those records | Separate missing, invalid, unknown and withheld values; do not treat them as opposition. |
| Nonresponse | Eligible invitees without a response divided by eligible invitees under a documented invitation protocol | “Unknown: no invitation frame” for an open browser stream. Visitors and national population are not substitute denominators. |

All percentages must expose numerator/denominator and the population definition
adjacent to the value or through an accessible description. A zero denominator
renders “No responses,” never 0% approval. A suppressed denominator must suppress
the corresponding percentage. Plan priority scores such as 28/100 are modeled
scores, not participation percentages; label the scale and its construction.
Keep sample, simulated, real-unverified, verified and unresolved legacy provenance
separate. No public combined total. Preserve original records and source markers;
any migration needs an idempotent manifest and human-reviewed legacy mapping.

For open participation, display “Self-selected responses; population approval and
margin of error cannot be estimated from these data.” Do not attach a binomial
confidence interval to imply population validity. If a justified survey track is
later added, require a documented target population, frame, recruitment design,
inclusion probabilities, weights, clustering/strata and nonresponse treatment.
Report estimator, confidence level, method, design effect and effective sample
size with versioned methodology; mark unavailable quantities unavailable. Design
effects cannot repair unknown selection bias in the browser stream.

## Privacy and explanation prototype

Prototype geographic publication with a provisional minimum of ten distinct
contributing tokens per released cell/subgroup, subject to privacy review. This
is a proposed publication rule, not a change to the existing k=3 journal rule or
a guarantee of anonymity. Apply suppression on the server to counts, percentages,
maps, tooltips, lists and exports. Use complementary suppression or withhold totals
when subtraction reveals a hidden count. Restrict overlapping filters, repeated
time snapshots and revision comparisons that permit differencing. Do not expose
identifiers, precise locations or suppressed-presence indicators in API payloads.
Keep real zero, unknown and withheld states distinct without revealing small-cell
membership. Publish only geography justified by collected coarse evidence; no
IP-derived county inference is proposed.

Provide an inline expandable “How these counts were obtained” panel on `/votes`
and `/map`, reachable by keyboard and readable without a tooltip. Proposed copy:

> These are voluntary browser responses, not a representative survey. One browser
> token can save a choice on this proposal; it does not verify a unique person or
> residency. Sample tallies and county simulations are shown separately. Geometry
> shows places we can draw, not places where someone participated. Some geographic
> results are withheld to protect privacy. We do not know how many eligible people
> saw the question and chose not to respond.

Include the scope/window, current numerator/denominator, origin, last update,
missingness and suppression explanation in the expanded panel. Simulation mode
must label map headings, metrics, legend, selected county and accessible map
description as modeled. Coordinate that wording with SHE-297. Do not let a
simulation disclosure below the fold carry the entire explanation.
