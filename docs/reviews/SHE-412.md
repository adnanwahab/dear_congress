# SHE-412 — budget-balancing policy puzzle

Refs: https://linear.app/shel-bernstein/issue/SHE-412/joyquest-budget-balancing-policy-puzzle

## Implementation and boundaries

The `/synth` civic stage now includes a demo sandbox awaiting review, before the
existing law explorer and advanced synthesis controls. Five fictional programs
have named goals and invented annual costs against a fixed $40B play budget.
Native labeled checkboxes and buttons support activation without dragging or
hovering. Every card names beneficiaries and a tradeoff; reviewing a feasible
plan also explains the groups whose goals are deferred. Two editable viable
examples illustrate different priorities, with neither presented as recommended.
Unallocated money is allowed, explicitly receives no assumed benefit, and can
represent other priorities or a reserve. Over-budget choices remain visible and
editable, but review is disabled until the plan fits. Reset starts again.

Arithmetic reuses integer-million `budgetUnits` and `budgetMoney` from the shared
fiscal ledger. Fictional `quest-*` IDs deliberately have no Proposal or ballot
binding. There is no data fetch, storage, publication, callback to synthesis or
simulator call. Choices live only in component memory and reset on unmount or
reload. Bundled fixtures have no asynchronous puzzle loading or remote failure;
the existing app loader/error boundary still handles route data/code loading.
Invalid selection arithmetic fails closed with a local reset action. The puzzle
adds no animation, timers, transitions, or dependence on motion preferences;
reduced motion gets the same complete text and controls. Cards reflow using the
existing theme and source badge styling; buttons have 44px minimum targets.
