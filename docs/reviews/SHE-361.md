## Implemented candidate

On November 13, 2026, replaced the default `/documentation` and `/docs` content
with a plain-English “How Civic Synthesis works” guide. It explains read/respond/
journal paths, independence from government, advisory browser choices, sample and
simulated data, publication visibility, extraction, deletion and retention limits,
source methodology, simulated outreach and measurement, real bill verification,
a glossary and feedback. Relative links keep the guide on its serving domain.
Privacy/account are existing routes; `/version` and `/healthz` provide build and
service availability, explicitly not policy success. No nonexistent About, terms
or status page is advertised. Background/feedback use the project's public GitHub
routes pending SHE-285. The existing 404 response gives a meaningful explanation
and recovery links; `/status` remains a 404 rather than a fabricated status page.

The original design report is preserved unchanged in a native, keyboard-operable,
collapsed historical appendix dated November 12, 2026. Its archived assertions
are explicitly not current commitments. The existing SHE-339 diagram remains
inside that appendix; its cross-app language is not used as current guidance.
Downloads now contain the public guide. Page metadata identifies the new guide.
