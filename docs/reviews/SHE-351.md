# SHE-351 — tactile control feedback

Refs: https://linear.app/shel-bernstein/issue/SHE-351/demomotion-tactile-hover-press-and-focus-states-on-buttons-cards-and

Implemented hover lift and pressed translation/scale for buttons, button links and
bottom navigation, plus hover elevation and child-control focus/press feedback on
stage tiles, source cards, Feed cards, citizen proposal cards and sponsor cards.
Text-link underlines transition color. Native child controls retain their semantics
and tab order. Disabled controls do not lift or press. Sponsor drag handles and
MapLibre controls retain their positioning; sponsor inline drag transforms do not
transition behind the pointer.

All legacy client CSS hover selectors are gated by `(hover: hover) and
(pointer: fine)`. New movement and elevation require
`prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; reduced motion retains color feedback
and accessible focus indicators. Interaction transitions use `--motion-fast` and
`--ease-out`. Focus uses a contrasting outline and halo; outline geometry stays
fixed to avoid movement, while outline color eases in. No layout properties are
animated.
