## Session protocol for the human facilitator

Recruit five distinct people who did not author, review or previously rehearse this flow. Do not substitute agents, five browser profiles, staff role-play or analytics for independent testers. Obtain consent for anonymous notes; use IDs T1–T5 and keep names/contact information outside the repository. Include mobile and desktop visitors and record relevant prior familiarity and accessibility needs voluntarily, without inferring residency or civic eligibility. Test separately; participants must not see earlier answers or receive coaching.

Use the actual promotion candidate on an isolated disposable-data preview with the same copy, build and configuration relevant to the flow. Record its SHA, date/time, URL, viewport/input method and fixture IDs. Confirm no production storage, outward messaging, repository ballot or merge path is connected. Compare the preview with the live entry surface read-only and record any differences; unresolved material differences prevent claiming production acceptance. Do not cast test votes, publish proposals or reset data on production. If a matching isolated preview is unavailable, record the flow as untested.

1. Open the clean entry page. Say only: “Please explore this site as you normally would. Tell me what you think it does.” Record the first click, destination, elapsed time and what they expected before explaining any labels. Allow skipping or stopping.
2. Ask exactly, before coaching: **“Is this government?” “Are tallies verified people?” “What happens after a vote?”** Record verbatim answers and uncertainty. Ask “What on the page led you to that?” without suggesting the correct answer.
3. Ask them to find a proposal, identify whether it is fictional, citizen-authored or an official record, and inspect its source. Include a fictional sample with an official background link and an actual source-backed official record where available. Ask whether the sample clause is itself an introduced or enacted bill and what the official reference establishes. If no official record is available, mark that distinction untested; do not relabel a sample as real.
4. In the isolated preview only, ask them to choose any stance or abstain, explain what they expect, attempt saving a choice, inspect confirmation, change it, remove it and reload. Record each attempt, observed saved state, errors, help needed and whether they confuse sample totals with their saved choice. Do not prescribe a political stance or reward a vote. A voluntary abstention is respected and recorded, not counted as a successful write attempt.
5. Repeat the three questions after the attempt, preserving both initial and later answers. Ask whether anything was sent to an office, changed law, proved eligibility or authorized a merge. Record what they believe about sponsor preparation and simulated outcomes. Debrief only after answers are captured; a taught answer is not an uncoached pass.

## Scoring and promotion decision

Score each initial and final answer separately as correct, uncertain, or incorrect; keep quotes alongside the score. Expected understanding: independent advisory beta, no government affiliation/official ballot; cookies and saved browser choices do not verify distinct people, residents or eligibility; illustrative sample totals are fictional; saving records a reversible preference in the prototype, with no law, spending, office delivery or merge authority. Official background records remain distinct from fictional proposals and citizen drafts.

**Any observed serious misrepresentation places promotion on HOLD immediately**, including belief that this is government/official voting, invented totals are real participants, browser identities verify people/eligibility, a sample is actual legislation because of its source link, or voting enacts law, sends outreach or merges code. Do not average these away with four correct answers. Log the trigger, affected route/revision and evidence; assign a human owner, prepare a specific fix, verify it on an isolated build and rerun an uncoached five-person check of the revised candidate. Preserve the original records and link the fix SHA and retest evidence. No automatic production or governance switch is changed by this document.

For this review's proposed gate, require five completed independent sessions on the candidate, correct uncoached trust distinctions, an attempted flow per participant, and no unresolved serious misrepresentation. Record uncertain answers as unresolved until reviewed. Minor navigation or visual polish may enter backlog without blocking a safe beta when a human documents why it does not obscure identity, provenance, consent or vote consequences. This small qualitative sample does not estimate population-wide comprehension or certify civic legitimacy. A human maintainer owns the final promotion decision.
