## Evidence cases and limits

- Sourced case: Census 2023 ACS one-year B08303 schema, specifically total estimate
  and margin-of-error variables. Source inspected 2026-11-14:
  https://api.census.gov/data/2023/acs/acs1/groups/B08303.html.
  This fully cited case concerns metric metadata, not a Harris County value.
  The county/route research question is separately labeled editorial inference.
- Conflicting case: explicitly fictional user-claim and synthetic-estimate pair.
  No real resident input or competing public measurements were invented or imported.
- Missing-source case: no registered step-free evening-trip observation. It does
  not assert no public dataset exists and never assigns a zero value.

Human review remains pending for every entry. Material changes require a new
revision/history event with date, author, reason and review state; this static
inventory provides no editing or approval endpoint. Real conflicting public
observations and a retrieved, archived county measurement still require a data
steward, source snapshots and independent verification. Do not treat the cited
schema or fictional conflict as complete real-outcome acceptance; leave the
Linear issue open for human verification.
