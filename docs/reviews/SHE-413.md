# SHE-413: fictional council practice

Refs: https://linear.app/shel-bernstein/issue/SHE-413/joyquest-city-council-roleplay-with-transparent-fictional-characters

Implemented a clearly labeled sandbox in WorkflowScreen's existing Votes stage.
Five one-minute rounds cover chair, budget officer, parent and transit user
responsibilities, competing budget needs and a reflection recommendation. Every
persona and response is scripted fiction. All dialogue, options and selected
responses remain readable in the full transcript, including after skipping.
Pause/resume, previous/next, skip and restart need no account or storage.
Selecting a response pauses the clock. Reload resets the exercise.
