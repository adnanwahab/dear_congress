## Implementation candidate

ChartChallenge supplies three invented learning fixtures: library visits
100→110 with a 90 baseline; workshop completions 40/50→60/100; and exhibit
responses 80/100 versus 20/100, combined 100/200. Scenes explicitly label both
the fictional source and the deliberately misleading claim. Reveals explain the
flaw, show zero-baseline corrections and numerical tables. A native disclosure
contains complete prose for every scene without requiring a correct answer.
Native buttons support guessing, reveal, skip, restart and exit; focus moves to
scene headings and returns to the launch button. Escape closes practice.

Opt-in entry points are in all three requested screens. The tour pauses on entry
and ignores its own keyboard shortcuts when the event target is in practice.
Practice state exists only in React memory. No persistence, service calls,
participation mutations, identity transfers, rewards or ideological scoring were
added. Existing source/repository/item identities, head/base bindings, votes,
records and receipts are untouched.
