## Exercise contract

Lantern Bay and all three stakeholders are fictional. Public constraints require
step-free access, evening opening and insulation if evenings open. The fixed
budget is twelve invented tokens. Access (4), evenings (5) and insulation (3)
form a feasible default compromise. Adding a garden (4) exceeds the budget.
Tavi still prefers a garden; feasibility never claims ideological convergence.
Each option exposes a concrete drawback. To finish, players must choose each
selected option's actual drawback rather than “No one gives anything up.” This
checks comprehension of toy rules, not the truth of real-world causal effects.

Assumptions can be edited independently; budget cannot be raised. Every package
or assumption edit clears prior acknowledgements and completion feedback. Reset
restores defaults. Empty packages never win, even when all constraints are off.
Budget and constraint failures remain visible and can be retried immediately.

The bundled synchronous fixture has no asynchronous loading/network failure
state. It remains usable without the separately loading simulator. Native labeled
checkboxes, selects, details and buttons support keyboard and touch, with 44px
control targets, wrapping text and live status feedback. No animation, timers,
autoplay, drag requirement or motion-dependent state was introduced. DOM tests
cover controls and feedback; actual browser, screen-reader and touch acceptance
remain for human review, as required by accessibility-acceptance.md.
