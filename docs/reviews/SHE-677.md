## Implemented

Outcomes now includes a read-only contradiction compass beside measurements.
Explicit date, definition, status and reported-value differences are detected
only within equal scope, period and claim kind. Both records remain visible;
no truth selection or voting authority is produced. Source identity, link,
revision, period, record date, limitations and pending human review accompany
claims and JSON exports. Initial material records include author/reason/review
history. No production record update path or human-review mutation is added.

Examples are a real primary-text citation, two explicitly invented conflicting
reports, and an unsupported user claim. The four claim categories are explained
in the compass and exported public guide. Synthetic reports have no fake source
URLs. Their conflict demonstrates the detector, not disagreement between two
verified official documents. Existing fictional measurement labels and scenario
source disclosures are preserved.

Comparison text renders immediately without a separate request; once the page
loads it has no online dependency. Original-document navigation may fail offline
and is explicitly labeled unverified. Native disclosures/buttons, responsive
columns, copyable JSON, download failure feedback and printed source URLs provide
fallbacks. No claims are fed to synthesis, journals, ballots or governance.
