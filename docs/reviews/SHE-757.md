## Fallback and rollback

Default is Standard navigation: no additional row. Choose Standard in More or
Participation & privacy to restore it. Back/Top remain in More and desktop utilities.
Storage failure retains the choice in page memory; it may reset on navigation or
reload. Without JavaScript ordinary destination links remain the fallback. On desktop
the additional group is hidden. No View Transition support is needed. A source
rollback removes the preference component and styles; its inert civic-reach storage
key contains no drafts or votes and may be deleted independently. Do not clear other
browser storage or production records.
