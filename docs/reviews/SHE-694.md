## Proposed durable review contract

Implement additive persistence and server authorization before exposing sign-off
controls. Preserve existing records and all advisory counts without migration into
review approvals. Do not retrofit inferred revisions or historical signatures.

1. Identify every artifact by source repository, item kind, source ID, artifact
   ID and immutable revision ID plus canonical content digest. A referenced PR
   also retains exact head and base. Same IDs in different repositories or kinds
   must remain distinct. A new draft revision starts a new review; prior reviews
   remain historical and visibly superseded, never transferred automatically.
2. Store legal fit, cost, empirical support, privacy and accessibility checks for
   each revision. Each check has pending, needs-changes or satisfied state, an
   explanation/evidence note and authorized reviewer attribution. Missing,
   unknown, disputed or needs-changes checks remain unresolved. All five must
   be satisfied to permit a signed approval; no unchecked default or silent
   not-applicable path may make an unresolved check green.
3. Legal fit records proposed jurisdiction/authority and unresolved questions;
   cost records estimates, assumptions and unknowns; empirical support records
   sources, limitations and contrary evidence; privacy records audience,
   retention and disclosure concerns; accessibility records actual test evidence
   and remaining barriers. These are reviewer assessments, not automatic legal
   certification or proof of empirical truth.
4. A sign-off records server-authenticated actor and role, exact revision/digest,
   decision, check snapshot, server timestamp and sequence. Approval is derived
   server-side only after validating every check and current authority. A request
   claiming approval with unresolved checks is rejected atomically. Separate
   request-changes and abstain decisions stay visible. Browser names, advisory
   choices, sample totals, synthetic participants and agent process success never
   become review authority or contributor counts.
5. Use expected revision and permission versions on writes. Reject stale sessions,
   changed drafts, changed PR bindings, expired credentials and revoked roles
   before committing. Persist action and append-only audit event atomically with
   idempotent retry and rollback on save failure. Reopening a check invalidates
   current approval through a new event; keep the original signed snapshot and
   reason. Signature withdrawal likewise appends rather than erases history.

## Audience, membership and moderation

Personal, invite-only and public scopes must have visible text labels and an
audience explanation on each artifact. Default new drafts to personal. Session
membership is an upper bound; it does not grant access to every artifact or widen
its scope. Require explicit per-artifact sharing confirmation, a preview of the
actual content and audience, and separate attribution consent. Public sharing
cannot recall previously downloaded copies; explain this before publication.

Bind invitations to authorized recipients; expire and revoke them. The server
must authorize reads, mutations, links, exports and every stream delivery. Avoid
leaking private titles, participants or counts through topic discovery, search,
error messages or URLs. Use no-store responses for restricted projections.
Browser privacy controls and local draft storage must retain their current scope.

Provide Leave session, Revoke artifact sharing, Withdraw my sign-off and Report
as distinct actions with explicit effects. Leaving removes membership access;
an authorized management path still permits the person to revoke their own
sharing/signature. Revocation blocks future reads and stream delivery, clears
cached UI content on revalidation, and retains only appropriately restricted
audit receipts. Withdrawal is not historical deletion. Define retention and
erasure rules for sensitive audit payloads without exposing withdrawn text.

Reports identify repository/kind/artifact/revision explicitly, accept bounded
reasons and enter a private moderator queue. Authorize moderator actions and
record reasons. A report alone neither grants access nor decides a review.
Hidden artifacts disappear from all relevant projections; authorized viewers may
see a neutral unavailable state without exposing text. Preserve source identities
and receipts. Reuse existing same-origin, signed-session, request size and rate
protections; extend their tests to the new endpoints.

## Integration sequence

- CitizenProposals: offer review preparation bound to the loaded citizen artifact
  and exact revision, with the five checks, missing evidence and decision history.
  Keep public publication separate from review sharing. No automatic draft upload
  or reinterpretation of existing comments/votes as sign-offs.
- LiveRound: retain advisory sample rounds and label their sample provenance.
  Link a review only through an explicit authorized artifact/session binding;
  do not infer attendees from voter hashes or equate round end with approval.
  Demo messages and synthetic attendees, if later added, need a separate visible
  demo lane and exclusion from real participant counts.
- TopicHubs: expose only explicitly public, moderated review artifacts with
  recorded topic-curation consent. Preserve sample/source lanes and avoid
  disclosure of invite-only or personal artifacts through summaries or counts.

Use semantic fieldsets and labels, text check states, keyboard-operable controls,
visible focus and status/error announcements. Wrap long revision IDs and source
notes on mobile. On session/artifact changes clear previous restricted state,
cancel pending requests and ignore late responses. Reconnect must reauthorize;
cached approval cannot remain current after a conflict or permission denial.
