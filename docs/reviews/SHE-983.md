## Delivered

The repository now has a public CSV ledger and operator guide under
`docs/launch-distribution/`, linked from the readme. It records placement identity,
channel, publish time, owner, approval, consent, destination, spend, aggregate
sessions, follow-through, measurement provenance and limitations. Append-only
superseding records preserve corrections without double-counting snapshots.
The guide distinguishes visits, sessions, identities and people, unknown from
zero, and compatible denominators from unsupported conversion rates.

The ledger is deliberately empty: no approved placements, consent evidence,
accepted owners, costs or aggregate reports were supplied. No fictional rows,
invented participation or assumed approvals are presented as operations evidence.
This is an operational recording artifact, not a new analytics service or UI.

## Acceptance remains open

A human owner must verify actual approved channels, record permission and
publication evidence, and enter dated aggregate reports using their real units.
If sessions/follow-through are unavailable, retain blanks and state the gaps.
The current site cannot automatically supply these channel metrics. New
instrumentation would need a separately reviewed privacy and attribution design.
Do not backfill from page views, votes, timing or guessed person counts.

Review the adjacent announcement/readout work before adopting common reporting
windows. Verify ledger entries and methodology before acceptance; a draft PR or
this recording format alone does not prove actual distribution or useful traffic.
Leave SHE-983 open for that evidence and human verification.
