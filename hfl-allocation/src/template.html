Anchors (sourced figures)

  1. A1, child credit (HFL-002): The Joint Committee on Taxation puts full refundability of the $2,000 credit for low-income families at about $12B a year through 2025. A Tax Policy Center model puts full refundability of the 2021-expanded credit at about $53B a year. The IRS issued nearly $94B of expanded credits for 2021.
  2. A2, EITC (HFL-003): The Joint Committee on Taxation puts total EITC cost at about $67B in 2025, and the temporary 2021 expansion for workers without children at about $12B.
  3. A3, school meals (HFL-036): Preliminary FY2024 figures put the National School Lunch Program at about $17.7B for 4.8B lunches, and the School Breakfast Program at about $5.7B for 2.5B breakfasts.
  4. A4, lead lines (HFL-091): EPA's average replacement cost is $4,700 per line, with a range from $1,200 to $12,300. A late-2025 EPA update counts about 4 million lead service lines, down from 9 million. CDM Smith, an engineering firm, puts the national average at $12,500 per line, slightly above the top of this range.
  5. A5, broadband (HFL-086): The BEAD program totals $42.45B.
  6. A6, error risk (HFL-002, HFL-003): For FY2022 the IRS put the EITC improper payment rate at 32%, with overpayments of about $18.2B against $57.5B in outlays. A later estimate for tax year 2022, from the FY2024 agency financial report, puts the rate at 27%.

Method

  • Low, Mid and High are the bottom, midpoint and top of each range. HFL-091's midpoint is set to $18.8B (about 4M lines at $4,700).
  • Annual is the recurring cost once a program runs, staff included. FTE is capacity and is not costed again.
  • Year-1 spend is the one-time cost plus the chosen share of annual cost.
  • Private compliance costs (employers, lenders, platforms) appear in the Watch-out column and are not totaled.
  • Not counted: benefit payouts to newly enrolled people (HFL-001), drug-price savings (HFL-029), avoided emergency and police costs (HFL-024), lost fine or land-sale revenue (HFL-007, HFL-014), and the local infrastructure that growth requires (HFL-011).
  • HFL-101 to HFL-110 (the international moonshot set) are not included.
